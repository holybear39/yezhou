关于岛的想像 #1：工作坊，行为表演，展览

Imaginaries of an Island #1: Workshop, Performance, and the Exhibition

地点 / Venue弘美术馆，温州 / Hong Museum, Wenzhou 年份 / Year2025 形式 / Format工作坊，行为表演，雕塑装置 / Workshop, Performance, Sculptural Installation

01 工作坊 Workshop

这次"做岛"由胶冻小组与十四位岛民在工作坊中共同完成。我们希望借此在城市里为参与的"自个儿"搭建一座精神呼吸空间，也希望让更多人去了解岩衣胶冻的发源地，北策岛，以及通过"做岛"的过程来理解老一辈水潭村民对北策的执着。

This time, the "Island-making" was realized collectively by Jelly Troupe and fourteen "islanders" during the workshop. Our intention was to create, within the city, a breathing space for the spirit of each participant, while also inviting more people to learn about the origin of Yanyi (Rockcoat) Jelly on Beice Island, and to understand—through the process of "making islands"—the older generation of Shuitan villagers' attachment to Beice.

大家分享自己带来物件的故事Participants sharing the stories of the objects they brought

做岛，需要岛民各带来自己的物品或捡来的东西（三件以上）用这些东西来拼贴和想象"岛"的功能和样貌。大家带来了书籍、家什、工具与私人珍藏。在工作坊的开始，我们席地而坐，分享自己和带来物件的故事，通过故事，我们看见了这些物件，并且相互认识。

To "make an island," each islander was asked to bring at least three objects of their own or items they had found. These objects were used to collage and imagine the island's functions and appearance. Participants brought books, household goods, tools, and personal treasures. At the start of the workshop, we sat on the floor and shared the stories behind our objects. Through these stories, we began to see the objects anew, and to know each other.

空间中的行走。用脚步去丈量，感受空间的形状Walking through the space, using footsteps to measure and feel its shape

在制作的过程中，这些物件组在一块儿成了微缩的世界，带着它们的故事和念想在小组的遐想里构筑起新的寄托与存在。小时候的玩具假人变成了岛上的女神像，一张未递出去的纸条成为了给登岛者的谜题，老家淘汰的饮水机成为了青春回忆的恒温室，在汽车上得到的塑料袋做成了岛上的树，给岛民乘凉，闲暇时候写下的歌词成为了岛民的读物……我们一共有81个物件，而每一个物件都在这儿遇上了它们的今天与昨日。

During the making, these objects gathered into a miniature world. Carried by their stories and memories, they built new forms of belonging in the group's imagination: a childhood toy figurine became a goddess statue; an unsent note became a riddle for newcomers to the island; an old water dispenser from home turned into a greenhouse of youth's memories; a plastic bag picked up from a car became a tree offering shade; song lyrics scribbled during idle time became reading material for islanders... Altogether, there were 81 objects, and each one encountered both its yesterday and today here.

相比脑海中的印象，生活方式与地理环境的改变更直接地作用于我们的身体感受。水潭村的海洋记忆随着海岸线的消失而远去，村民将捕鱼的记忆放在了远处停滞的北策岛上。当城市居民的当地记忆因时间，或搬迁而无处容身时，并没有一个远处的岛屿来去承载。

Compared to impressions in memory, changes in lifestyle and geography affect our bodily perception more directly. In Shuitan Village, oceanic memory has faded with the receding coastline, while the villagers placed their fishing memories onto the distant, motionless Beice Island. When urban residents' local memories find no shelter—whether due to time or relocation—there is no faraway island left to hold them.

但"做岛"也不仅是一次对城市空间，地方与个人历史的回应，同时它是一次回归自身，再融入集体创造的体验。在来到选择要带来的物品的时候见到了自己，来到了美术馆时则遇见了他人。

Yet "Island-making" is not only a response to urban space, local culture, and personal history—it is also an experience of returning to oneself and then merging into collective creation. In choosing what to bring, one encounters oneself; upon arriving at the museum, one encounters others.

在工作坊的结尾，大家互相参观各组的岛屿，品尝了来自水潭村的岩衣胶冻。At the end of the workshop, participants visited each group's islands and tasted Yanyi Jelly brought from Shuitan Village.

02 行为表演 Performance

当晚，我们在岛屿的陪伴下表演了胶冻小组这次驻地中和水潭村的相遇。演出与工作坊在白天，黑夜的时间线上合作，小组成员则跨越了太平洋，在岛屿，胶冻，个人历史等主题上与空间里的物件遥相呼应，在过程中相互完整，算是胶冻小组在这个夏天以来，与温州最近的一次距离。

That evening, in the company of the islands, we performed Jelly Troupe's encounter with Shuitan Village during this residency. The performance and workshop unfolded across day and night; members of the troupe, spread across the Pacific, resonated remotely with the objects in the space, weaving together themes of islands, jelly, and personal histories. In this process, we completed one another—a moment of closeness between Jelly Troupe and Wenzhou this summer.

03 展览 Exhibition

在这次的工作坊中，大家一共做了三座"岛屿"，《希望之岛》，《女神岛》，《无名岛》。这三座岛以及它们的故事，卡密尔的行为指示谱将在美术馆中展出一个月，届时它们会安静的待在那里，让大家去读一读身上的每一个物件与痕迹。

During this workshop, we created three islands: Island of Hope, Goddess Island, and Nameless Island. These three islands, together with their stories and Camille's performance scores, will remain on view in the museum for one month. They will quietly stay there, waiting for visitors to read the marks and objects inscribed upon them.

欢迎和三座岛一起坐一下，小憩一会儿。你可以闭上眼来看，那样也许你会听到这些岛屿的声音，也许会慢下来，去回忆，去用你与自己的连接、与你认识或不认识的人、你爱或不爱的人之间的关系，在之后的某个时间，建造一座属于你们的纪念岛。

You are welcome to sit with the three islands, to rest a while. You may close your eyes—perhaps then you will hear the voices of these islands. Perhaps you will slow down, recall memories, and through your connection with yourself, with people you know or not, with those you love or not, build—at some future moment—a memorial island of your own.

关于岛的想象 #2，2025，温州

Imaginaries of an Island #2, 2025, Wenzhou
Participatory Installation, Wenzhou Kean University Library

《关于岛的想象 #2》是首次"关于岛的想象"展览的延续。它漂流自第一期工作坊参与者带来的故事与物件,又汇入第二期工作坊中温州肯恩大学学生讲述的故事。展览已在弘美术馆持续展出三个月,本次的装置是其更加完整的形态。

展览以温州北策岛上的"岩衣胶冻"为对象,呈现一个通过"关于岛的想象"系列工作坊生成的仪式化纪念装置。它献给"岩衣胶冻",也献给其背后逐渐消散的渔民空间与社群,以及一同制作的参与者、艺术家们和大家留下的场景。

展览中的彩色围墙,材质是具有胶冻质感的软膜;展出的物件,是两期工作坊中胶冻小组与参与者共同创作的作品。"关于岛的想象"致敬水潭村上一代人的坚韧、灵动与生机,也致敬人们在生活中彼此依靠、共同面对现实的方式。通过留出一片玩乐、聆听与共同创造的空间,小组希望鼓舞大家对自身形态与色彩叙事权的想象。

本次艺术实践由胶冻小组与 Maggie、竹妍、杨畅、刘凯璐、杏也、李彤彤、柯琼苗、何安若、张芑、赵依诺、何欧苗、丁家辉、朱孜然、张日辰,以及参与温州肯恩大学工作坊场次的张嘉睿、张易峰、王艺瞳、周靖乔、鲍飞海、郑从越、谢怿、汤涟猗共同创作,并在弘美术馆与温州肯恩大学图书馆的支持下完成。

Imaginaries of an Island #2 is a continuation of the first Imaginaries of an Island exhibition. Drifting with stories and objects from participants in the first workshop, it takes in new stories told during the second workshop with students from Wenzhou-Kean University. The exhibition has been on view at Hong Art Museum for three months, and this installation is its more complete form.

Focusing on “Rock-Skin Jelly” (岩衣胶冻) on Beice Island, Wenzhou, the exhibition presents a ritual memorial installation generated through the Imaginaries of an Island workshop series. It is dedicated to the Rock-Skin Jelly, to the fishing community and communal spaces that have faded behind it, and to the scenes left behind by the participants, artists, and everyone who made it together.

The colorful walls in the exhibition are made of a soft film with a jelly-like texture. The objects on display were created jointly by The Jelly Troupe and participants across the two workshops. Imaginaries of an Island pays tribute to the resilience, ingenuity, and vitality of the previous generation in Shuitan Village, and to the ways people lean on one another and face reality together in daily life. By holding open a space for play, listening, and co-creation, the troupe hopes to encourage imagination about each person’s own right to narrate their form and color.

This artistic practice was created by The Jelly Troupe together with Maggie, Zhu Yan, Yang Chang, Liu Kailu, Xing Ye, Li Tongtong, Ke Qiongmiao, He Anruo, Zhang Qi, Zhao Yinuo, He Oumiao, Ding Jiahui, Zhu Ziran, Zhang Richen, and, from the Wenzhou-Kean University workshop session, Zhang Jiarui, Zhang Yifeng, Wang Yitong, Zhou Jingqiao, Bao Feihai, Zheng Congyue, Xie Yi, and Tang Lianyi. It was realized with support from Hong Art Museum and the Wenzhou-Kean University Library.

水边踏踏步，2026

Marching by the Water, 2026

-->

我受山寨MFA邀请，在上海郊区的水乡朱家角举办一场活动，作为其为期一个月的「山寨水会」项目的一部分。 「水边踏踏步」是一场特定场域的行为表演。参与艺术家沿着水道结伴行走，并共同完成一场集体演出。这场表演重新演绎了圣琼·佩斯的长诗《远征》（Anabasis），并以行为艺术的语言，对长征这一贯穿诗作、反复出现在中国近现代历史中的主题，进行诗性而富有游戏意味的注解。

I was invited by Shanzhai MFA (山寨MFA) to host an event in Zhujiajiao, a water town on the outskirts of Shanghai, as part of Shanzhai Water Gathering (山寨水会), their month-long program. Step Step by the Water (水边踏踏步) is a site-specific performance in which participating artists walk together along the waterway and take part in a collective performance. It is both a re-enactment of Saint-John Perse’s Anabasis and a poetic, playful annotation, in the language of performance art, of the Long March, a theme that runs through the poem and recurs throughout China’s modern and contemporary history.

规则 / Rules

20个由长诗《Anabasis》改编的谱（Scores）行为沿村展开。大家（1-3人一组）自由认领（每组需要认领2-3个指示）并选择各自的位置进行即兴的行为表演，并一同决定表演的开始和结束地点。每组表演最长时间为10分钟。上一组需要为下一组报幕。

Twenty scores for actions, adapted from the long poem Anabasis, unfold along the village. Participants, in groups of 1–3, freely claim instructions (each group claims 2–3), choose their own positions, perform improvised actions, and together decide where the performance begins and ends. Each group performs for 10 minutes at most. The preceding group introduces the next group.

冬天不过三天雨，夏天不过三天晴，2026，温州

Winter rain never lasts more than three days;
summer's clear skies never last more than three days, 2026, Wenzhou

受abC艺术节的邀请，胶冻小组将 《关于岛的想像 #2》搬到了温州园博园的公共艺术分会场， 并邀请温州非遗美食岩衣胶冻的传承人王爱琴女士一起来分会场卖岩衣胶冻。 在展览现场展览的五天时间里，胶冻小组邀请观众一块用装置的彩色板子来搭建出不同样子。

Invited by abC Art Festival, Jelly Troupe brought Imaginaries of an Island #2 to the Public Art section of the Wenzhou Garden Expo Park. The group also invited Ms. Wang Aiqin, an inheritor of Wenzhou’s intangible cultural heritage food tradition, Yanyi Jelly (岩衣胶冻), to join them at the venue and sell the local delicacy. Over the five days of the exhibition, Jelly Group invited visitors to use the colorful panels from the installation to build and assemble different forms together.

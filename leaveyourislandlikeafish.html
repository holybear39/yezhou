像鱼一样离开你的岛

Leave your island like a fish, 2026

Leave your island like a fish, 2026
Participatory Installation, Barefoot Local Art Festival, Xiamen

《变成鱼离开你的岛》是一件参与式装置作品。悬挂的蓝色透明帘子在展览空间中围出一个房间,房间内摆放着一套桌椅和一盏灯,桌上放有温州水潭渔村的老照片、关于水潭村的《胶冻小报》,以及彩色马克笔。作品邀请观众在装置上一同绘画、书写自己正在离开或准备离开的地方。这个地方可以是具体的地理位置,也可以是某种熟悉的生活状态、某个时间点、某个阶段, 一个坐标… 迁徙运动永远不会结束,因为任何"到达"都无法与迁徙动机中那个想象出来的"回归"相吻合。《变成鱼》以仪式性的方式纪念"离开"这一动作。"岛"是陆地,人在离开"岛"的同时,也变成了一条鱼。我们都处在变化的过程之中,你可能在还没有察觉时,就已经变成了一条鱼。作品邀请观众正视那些变化,以及与离开有关的记忆:坐在这个房间里,思考自己的离开,再去阅读这个渔村在过去三十年里如何告别自己过去的身份。 本作品由赤脚艺术节和弘美术馆委任,是胶冻小组《关于岛的想象》系列作品之四。

Leave Your Island Like a Fish is a participatory installation. Suspended translucent blue curtains enclose a room within the exhibition space. Inside are a table, a chair, and a lamp. On the table lie old photographs of Shuitan, a fishing village in Wenzhou, copies of the Jelly Gazette about Shuitan, and colored markers. Visitors are invited to draw and write on the installation about a place they are leaving or preparing to leave. This place can be a specific geographic location, or a familiar way of living, a moment in time, a stage of life, or something else… (the original sentence is unfinished here) The movement of migration never ends, because no "arrival" can ever match the imagined "return" that forms part of the motive for migrating in the first place. The work commemorates the act of leaving through ritual. The "island" is land, and the moment a person leaves it, they become a fish. We are all in the process of change, and you may have become a fish before you even realize it. The work invites visitors to acknowledge these changes and their memories of departure. They can sit in the room and reflect on their own leaving, then read about how this fishing village has parted with its past identities over the last thirty years. The work was commissioned by the Barefoot Art Festival and the Hong Art Museum. It is the fourth piece in the Imagining Islands series by the Jelly Group.

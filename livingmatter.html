Living Matter

与表演艺术家 Kyriakos Apostolidis 的表演二人组，2022–2025
镜头与身体运动关系的研究
Performance Duo with Kyriakos Apostolidis, 2022–2025
A Study of Camera in Relation with Body Movement

（Living Matter 截图）
(Screenshot of Living Matter)

Living Matter 是一场介于行为艺术与扩展电影之间的现场多媒体表演。作品呈现一个身体行动:表演者穿越空间,同时多路实时画面呈现表演者行动的演变,并合成为一个整合的影像。行动结束时,表演的不同阶段被同时投影出来,将表演空间转变为一件视频装置。

Living Matter is a live multimedia performance at the intersection of performance art and expanded cinema. The piece presents a physical action in which the performer crosses the space while multiple real-time live feeds show the evolution of performer’s action creating an integrated image. At the end of the action, the different phases of the performance are projected simultaneously transforming the performance space into a video installation.
